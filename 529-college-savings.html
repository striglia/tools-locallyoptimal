Notes & Citations

College Cost Inflation (5% default): Historically, college costs have increased at approximately 5-6% annually, roughly double the general inflation rate. From 2000-2020, average college costs increased 169% while general inflation was only 53%.[4] This trend has moderated slightly in recent years but remains significantly above general inflation.

[1] UC System (2024-25): Total cost of attendance (tuition, room & board, fees, books) for California residents is approximately $36,000/year at UC Berkeley and UCLA. Source: UC Office of the President, 2024-25 Cost of Attendance.
[2] Out-of-State Public (2024-25): Total cost of attendance for out-of-state students ranges from $48,000-54,000/year. University of Michigan: ~$54,000/year; University of Virginia: ~$50,000/year. Sources: Individual university financial aid offices, 2024-25 academic year.
[3] Private California (2024-25): Total cost of attendance at private universities ranges from $80,000-90,000/year. Stanford University: ~$87,000/year; USC: ~$85,000/year. Sources: Stanford Financial Aid Office, USC Financial Aid Office, 2024-25 academic year.
[4] College Cost Inflation Data: College Board, "Trends in College Pricing and Student Aid" (annual reports 2000-2024); Georgetown University Center on Education and the Workforce, "The College Payoff" (2021); National Center for Education Statistics (NCES) Digest of Education Statistics.

Note: All costs are estimates for planning purposes. Actual costs vary by institution, residency status, and individual circumstances. These figures represent total cost of attendance including tuition, fees, room, board, books, and personal expenses. Financial aid and scholarships can significantly reduce out-of-pocket costs.
