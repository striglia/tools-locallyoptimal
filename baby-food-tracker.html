Key Guidelines (AAP & Solid Starts)

  • Start around 6 months when baby shows signs of readiness
  • Introduce allergens early - eggs, peanuts, dairy, fish, tree nuts, wheat, soy, sesame
  • Prioritize iron-rich foods - meat, eggs, beans, fortified cereals
  • Avoid: honey (until 12 months), whole nuts, added salt/sugar, cow's milk as drink
