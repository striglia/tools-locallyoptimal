Notes & Assumptions

PMI: Private Mortgage Insurance is estimated at 0.5% of the loan amount annually when down payment is less than 20%. PMI is removed once equity reaches 20% of the original home price. Actual PMI rates vary by credit score and loan-to-value ratio (typically 0.3%-1.5%). CFPB: What is PMI?
Property Tax: California's base property tax rate is 1% (Prop 13), plus local assessments. SF County effective rate is approximately 1.18%. SF Treasurer: Property Taxes
Monthly Payment Formula: M = P[r(1+r)n] / [(1+r)n - 1], where P = principal, r = monthly rate, n = total payments. CFPB: Owning a Home
Closing Costs: Included in total cost calculations. Typical closing costs range from 2-5% of the loan amount. CFPB: Closing
OCR Upload: Loan estimate parsing uses Tesseract.js for optical character recognition. Supports PNG/JPG images of CFPB-standardized loan estimate documents. Extracted fields should be verified manually.

This calculator provides estimates for comparison purposes only. Actual mortgage terms, rates, and costs will vary. Consult a mortgage professional for precise quotes. Does not account for points or potential rate changes on adjustable-rate mortgages.
